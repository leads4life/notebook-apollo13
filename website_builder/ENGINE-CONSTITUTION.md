# BUSINESS WEBSITE COMPOSER R3 — ENGINE CONSTITUTION

## Mission
Build deployable business websites without forcing every business through one visual template.

## Modes

### ORIGINAL
Use when the business needs a new design language. Start from business reality, industry research, customer intent, brand position, content needs, and image strategy. Layout and visual system may be newly composed.

### RETROFIT
Use when an approved source/reference has unusually strong composition or design DNA worth preserving. Preserve the useful visual grammar while changing business-world imagery, content, conversion logic, terminology, SEO architecture, forms, proof, and customer journey.

### AUTO
- approved reference/baseline supplied -> RETROFIT
- no approved reference -> ORIGINAL
- ambiguous cases -> prefer ORIGINAL to prevent accidental template cloning

## No-drift contract
Strong capabilities may be added; previously validated capabilities must not silently disappear. R2 Retrofit remains intact inside R3.

## Original-mode requirements
1. Business-specific first fold and navigation.
2. Page architecture must match customer decision needs, not a fixed page count.
3. Category-native imagery and typography.
4. Local production images; source provenance recorded upstream.
5. Content decision-mass over word-count padding.
6. Page-family layout diversity.
7. Clear phone/form/visit/buy/booking paths as appropriate to the business.
8. Technical SEO basics: unique titles/descriptions, semantic headings, canonical strategy, schema where appropriate, sitemap/robots for multi-page sites.
9. No fabricated reviews, awards, licenses, results, addresses, or credentials.

## Retrofit-mode requirements
R2 contracts remain authoritative, including showcase-first proof, reimagination, local-image, truth, SEO/PPC, and Atlantic Meridian baseline grammar when that baseline is selected.

## Visual Truth Gate
Automated metrics are supporting evidence only. A page passes only when rendered pixels confirm:
- first-fold composition works
- headline/CTA are readable
- images are actually visible and correctly cropped
- no mobile clipping or component overflow
- no dead/empty sections
- page-family layouts do not collapse into one repeated template
- motion/effects do not hide essential content
